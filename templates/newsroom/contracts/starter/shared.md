## Voice

<< Path or URL to the org's public voice guide. >>

*A pointer, not a copy. If there is no guide, write three sentences here on

## Who reviews

Nothing the newsroom writes is the org's position until a person named in
`config.yml` under `review.reviewers` approves it. Drafts say so in their first
line.
